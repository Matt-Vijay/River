# River

Texas Hold'em with friends, in iMessage or around one iPhone.

<p>
  <img src="docs/images/lobby.jpg" alt="River lobby with five players ready to start" width="260">
  <img src="docs/images/table.jpg" alt="A five-player hand with private cards and betting controls" width="260">
</p>

- 2-5 players. No-limit betting. Play chips only.
- Choose a name and character, saved on your device.
- Send a table in Messages. Tap it to take a seat and play.
